This study investigates a novel approach to causal inference, aiming to maintain treatment effect estimation accuracy despite design shifts. The hypothesis posits that the proposed method can effectively estimate treatment effects across varying designs and covariate distributions. To validate this, we will simulate a dataset with different treatment policies and covariate distributions, testing the model's performance across these designs. An ablation test will assess the model's robustness by excluding specific covariates. We expect the model to demonstrate resilience in treatment effect estimation across design shifts. However, potential failure modes include confusing correlation with causation and neglecting the impact of unobserved confounders. Importantly, this research does not claim that the proposed method is the definitive solution for all causal inference challenges.
