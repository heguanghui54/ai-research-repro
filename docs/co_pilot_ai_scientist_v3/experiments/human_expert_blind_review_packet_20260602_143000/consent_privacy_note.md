# Consent and Privacy Note

This note is for reviewers who participate in the blind evaluation of
regenerated OpenReview mini-paper artifacts.

## What You Are Asked To Do

You will review six anonymized A/B artifact pairs. For each pair, you will
score Variant A and Variant B on a 1-5 rubric and choose `A`, `B`, or `tie`.

## What Is Collected

The project collects:

- anonymized reviewer ID,
- pair-level scores,
- A/B/tie winner choices,
- short rationales.

The project does not require:

- your legal name,
- email address in the score sheet,
- affiliation,
- demographic information.

## How Ratings Are Used

Ratings are used only to estimate whether paper-specific expert review feedback
improves regenerated research artifacts beyond matched-length unrelated review
context. Results should be reported in aggregate, including win counts, mean
score deltas, confidence intervals, and inter-rater agreement when enough
ratings are available.

## Blinding

The condition key is hidden from reviewers. Please do not try to infer which
condition produced a given artifact.

## Limits

This is a small research evaluation, not a formal peer-review process. The
artifacts are generated mini-paper proposals and are not complete papers.
