# Recruitment Email Template

Subject: Request for a 20-40 minute blind review of AI-generated ML paper artifacts

Hi [Name],

I am preparing a research project on human-in-the-loop automated science. The
specific question is whether real paper-specific expert review feedback improves
short regenerated research artifacts beyond equal amounts of unrelated review
context.

Would you be willing to spend about 20-40 minutes doing a blind review of six
short A/B artifact pairs? Each pair comes from an ML/AI paper. You will see the
source title/abstract excerpt and two anonymized generated artifacts. Please
score both artifacts with a fixed rubric and choose A, B, or tie.

You will not be asked to evaluate the original paper's acceptance decision, and
you should not try to infer which generation condition produced each artifact.
The goal is to judge artifact quality: problem framing, method specificity,
experiment design, limitation honesty, claim calibration, and overall quality.

Materials:

- `reviewer_index.md`
- `instructions.md`
- `pairs/pair_01.md` through `pairs/pair_06.md`
- `score_sheet_template.csv`

Please return only the completed CSV. Your ratings will be analyzed in
aggregate with anonymized reviewer IDs such as `R1`, `R2`, and `R3`.
