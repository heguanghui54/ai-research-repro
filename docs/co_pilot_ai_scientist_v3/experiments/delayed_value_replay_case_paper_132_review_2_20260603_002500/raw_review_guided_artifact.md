This research aims to deepen the understanding of Averaged Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) within the Neural Tangent Kernel (NTK) framework by investigating its generalization capabilities in finite-width networks. The hypothesis suggests that the generalization ability of averaged SGD can be significantly influenced by the width of the neural network. Building on the original paper's findings, we will analyze the minimax-optimal learning rates and conduct experiments to compare the generalization performance of averaged SGD across various network widths. An ablation test will be performed using a dataset with noisy labels to evaluate the robustness of averaged SGD in real-world scenarios. We anticipate that our findings will reveal critical insights into the interplay between network width and generalization, providing empirical support for the theoretical predictions made in the original study. However, we will ensure that our claims remain within the bounds of our experimental design, avoiding overgeneralization of the results.
