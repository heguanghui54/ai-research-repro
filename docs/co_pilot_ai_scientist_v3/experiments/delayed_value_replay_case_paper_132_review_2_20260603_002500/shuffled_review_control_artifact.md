This research investigates the performance of Averaged Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) across various network architectures and training dynamics, aiming to understand its generalization capabilities. The hypothesis suggests that the generalization performance of averaged SGD can be significantly influenced by the choice of network architecture and the dynamics of the training process. To explore this, we will conduct comparative analyses of averaged SGD performance on different architectures, evaluating how variations in training dynamics affect generalization. An ablation test will involve experimenting with diverse training data distributions to assess the robustness of averaged SGD. We anticipate that our findings will provide valuable insights into the interplay between network design, training dynamics, and generalization performance. However, we will ensure that our claims remain within the bounds of our experimental design, avoiding overgeneralization of the results.
