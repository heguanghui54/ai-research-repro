# Co-Pilot AI Scientist v3 Task Spec

## Research Task ID

`live_skill_invocation_001`

## Topic
Testing whether review-derived evaluator-stress gates reduce metric-gaming in an automated ML benchmark.

## Success Criteria
- Primary benchmark metric: Reduction in metric-gaming incidents.
- Minimum acceptable improvement: 15% reduction in metric-gaming incidents compared to baseline.
- Paper-quality target: Acceptance at a top-tier ML conference.
- Reproducibility target: 95% reproducibility of results across multiple runs.
- Taste/insight target:
  - Which non-metric scientific factor matters most? Novelty of the approach.
  - What high-tail outcome would justify preserving a risky branch? Discovery of a novel metric that significantly improves evaluation integrity.

## Failure Criteria
- What result would make the hypothesis unsupported? No significant reduction in metric-gaming incidents.
- What evaluator flaw would invalidate the run? Inconsistent evaluation criteria across runs.
- What budget limit stops the branch? Exceeding a budget of 100 compute hours.

## Human Gates
- `scientific_taste_prior`: To assess the initial hypotheses and their potential impact on the research.
- `evaluator_stress_test`: To validate the robustness of the evaluation metrics against metric-gaming.
- `frontier_steering`: To explore alternative hypotheses and directions based on initial findings.
- `verifiable_micro_evolution`: To iteratively refine the evaluation methods based on feedback.
- `claim_calibration`: To ensure that claims made in the paper are supported by the evidence gathered.

## Baselines
- Autonomous AI Scientist-v2 style run: Standard evaluation without human intervention.
- Direct LLM rewrite or sampling baseline: A baseline generated by a language model without human input.
- Existing method or benchmark baseline: Comparison against established benchmarks in the field.

## Expected Artifacts
- Human gate logs: Records of decisions made at each gate.
- Experiment logs: Detailed logs of each experimental run.
- Program-search traces: Traces of the program evolution process.
- Paper draft: Initial draft of the research paper.
- Reproducibility manifest update: Documentation of reproducibility efforts and results.
