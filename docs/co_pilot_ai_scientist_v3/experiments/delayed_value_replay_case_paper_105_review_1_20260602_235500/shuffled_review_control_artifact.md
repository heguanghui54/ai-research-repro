This study proposes a new approach to 3D medical image segmentation, focusing on improving both efficiency and accuracy. The hypothesis suggests that innovative model design can yield significant advancements in segmentation tasks. The methodology involves developing a new segmentation model and evaluating its performance against traditional approaches on a medical imaging dataset. An ablation study will be conducted to isolate the contributions of specific model components. Expected results include enhanced segmentation accuracy with optimized computational resource usage. However, potential failure modes may arise from overfitting and insufficient consideration of deployment scenarios. The study will not claim universal superiority over existing methods but will aim to demonstrate the new approach's advantages.
