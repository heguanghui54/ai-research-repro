# Positioning

Co-Pilot AI Scientist v3 is a research co-pilot skill built around
Insight-Gated Research Evolution (IGRE).

## One-Sentence Position

IGRE turns human scientific taste into six auditable gates inside automated
research workflows.

## Who It Is For

- ML/AI researchers using Codex or similar coding agents for research work.
- Students and labs that want to log where human judgment changes an automated
  research trajectory.
- Builders of AI Scientist-style systems who need claim calibration and
  evaluator-stress safeguards.

## What Makes It Different

Many academic skill projects help with writing, reviewing, citation checking,
or document preparation. IGRE focuses on the control points before and during
scientific search:

- Which direction should receive research budget?
- Is the evaluator measuring the scientific target or a shortcut?
- Should the agent continue the metric-best branch or a high-upside branch?
- Is a subproblem machine-gradeable enough for program evolution?
- Which review comments change the research trajectory?
- What claim is actually supported?

## Relation To Academic Research Skills

`academic-research-skills` is a high-adoption reference for packaging academic
work into reusable skills. It is especially strong as a broad research-to-
publication workflow. Co-Pilot AI Scientist v3 learns from that engineering
model, but it is narrower and more method-specific: it packages the six IGRE
gates for collaborative automated science.

## Boundary

Engineering adoption is not scientific superiority. A popular skill can prove
that the workflow is useful and transferable, but performance claims still need
matched autonomous baselines, benchmark evidence, and blind expert review.
